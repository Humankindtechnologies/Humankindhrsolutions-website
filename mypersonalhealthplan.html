Spotlight

What is Assured Access?

Group health benefits or previous plans can be lost for multiple reasons — career changes, a long-term disability, a leave of absence, or retirement. With Assured Access, you don't have to worry about leaving your health coverage behind when you change jobs, leave a job, or retire.

How Does it Work?

It takes a snapshot of your health situation today and guarantees your access to a personal health plan that would cover any new medical conditions that develop after that time. If you're concerned about the loss of your previous plan's health benefits, Assured Access can provide long-term security for you and your family.

What's included with Assured Access

More than future-proof coverage — real support and benefits from day one.

A Full Wellness Program

Access to inConfidence — our confidential assistance program offering one-on-one counselling and support, 24 hours a day, by phone and in person.

Cash Benefit

If you or an eligible dependent suffers an accident resulting in accidental death or dismemberment, Assured Access provides a cash benefit of up to $10,000, to spend at your discretion.

Waived Waiting Periods

If your other coverage is ending, waiting periods for health (e.g. vision and hearing aids), hospital, and even dental benefits can be waived.

Tax & HSA Friendly

Premiums can be added to your medical expenses when calculating tax credits, and can qualify toward your group Health Spending Account.

What's Covered

Comprehensive dental benefits

Essential dental services covered at 70% of your dentist's fees, based on your provincial Dental Society fee guide.

Preventive Services

Regular exams, X-rays, and cleanings to keep your teeth healthy before problems start.

Restorative Services

Fillings, extractions, root canal therapy, and surgical services — the work that keeps your mouth healthy.

Denture Services

Coverage for denture services so you have access to the full range of dental care you need.

All services subject to applicable annual limits. A 6-month waiting period applies if you are not applying within 60 days of losing group benefits.
